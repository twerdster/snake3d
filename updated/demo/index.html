Steering controls

← → turn left and right.
↑ ↓ tilt up and down.
Space pauses the game.

On a touch screen, hold and drag inside the arena to steer. Release to keep the current heading.

Ready. The first food is straight ahead.

The enclosure is a 3D volume. The view follows the snake; orange markers show the food.

New implementation by Aaron Wetzler with agent assistance (2026), inspired by Sapir Eltanani and Simona Gluzman's 2016 project. The original Unity/C#, Leap Motion, RealSense and Oculus code remains missing. This demo uses keyboard and touch controls, with no VR or hand tracking. It does not reproduce the historical body-mesh algorithm or establish device compatibility.
